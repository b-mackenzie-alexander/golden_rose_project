### Summary of Important Terms and Requirements
**Receipts:** The Agency will provide a separate, written receipt for every deposit, fee, or charge collected by it.

**Fee Amounts:** Agency cannot accept a deposit or charge a fee before you are paid by the employer.

**Legitimate Employment:** Agency will only send you to current, legitimate job openings obtained from the employer, unless they tell you otherwise in advance of being referred.

**Work Hours:** Agency will provide prior-to-placement information about: (1) hours per week expected to work; (2) whether paid weekly, biweekly, or monthly; and (3) known health/safety risks and preventive/control steps.

## Relation to the current contract/receipt evidence
- The content overlaps with the contract packet’s work/fee terms and applicant-fee notice, but this sheet labels itself nonbinding.
- It reinforces that the future platform must distinguish at least three separate document types: nonbinding terms/disclosures, executed applicant agreement, and issued receipt.
- They require different legal approvals, delivery/acknowledgement rules, audience, retention, correction, and DocuSign treatment. They must not be merged into a generic “contract” template merely because fields overlap.

## Candidate product implications — pending legal and business approval
1. A separate controlled template/document type for applicant terms/disclosures, with source, owner, approved version, effective date, applicability scope, and language/accessibility variant.
2. A delivery event and, where required, acknowledgement/access evidence. Do not assume a signature is legally required or that electronic delivery is permitted for every notice.
3. Field values should be drawn from approved job-order, placement, and fee-rule data—not manually retyped independently into each document.
4. Approved legal wording and the official business-plan payer/fee policy must determine whether/when this sheet is generated, who receives it, and whether the applicant can see or accept it electronically.
5. Attached legal references such as §§185–186 must remain controlled primary-source/versioned legal content, not static copied images.
