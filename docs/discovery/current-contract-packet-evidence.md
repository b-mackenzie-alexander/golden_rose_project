## Provisional document classification
**Strong inference, not client-confirmed:** this is an **applicant/worker placement agreement packet**, not an employer-client agreement.

Evidence supporting that interpretation:
- Its first substantive section is **Applicant Information** and its final page is headed **“NOTICE TO JOB APPLICANT — READ BEFORE SIGNING.”**
- The party signature lines are **Applicant Signature** and **Employment Agency Representative Signature**; there is no employer signature line in the photographed agreement pages.
- The packet describes conditions under which the agency may charge the **job applicant** and refers to placement/referral into work.
- Employer details are captured as job/placement facts, not as an employer’s executed agreement.

This remains a classification hypothesis until Golden Rose confirms the packet’s use, audience, and full document set.

## Candidate data model / UI inputs suggested by the evidence
These are **candidate fields only**; retain, remove, rename, and validate them only after client/counsel review.

1. **Agency record:** legal/display name, DCWP license number, office contact details, staff/representative identity.
2. **Applicant identity/contact:** name, phone, email, address. Avoid collecting additional identity data merely because a form exists; follow the data inventory and minimization process.
3. **Employer/job-order data:** legal/business contact, authorized hiring person, job/worksite address(es), verified-contact evidence, current-opening status.
4. **Work terms:** role/classification candidate, status, rate/compensation, hours/day, schedule, expected duration, transportation terms, work conditions, benefits/costs, accommodation/health-and-safety handling under a separate reviewed policy.
5. **Fee decision/case:** payer, legal-rule version, payment basis, calculated/approved amount, installment terms, receipt events, refund/void/correction events. This must not be a generic price field or client-editable legal exception.
6. **Document execution:** template/version, explicit required-field completeness check, signer role, signature/delivery/completion evidence, completed document, and retention/legal-hold state.
