# Office Observations — Golden Rose Employment Agency

**Status:** Reported observation from Beatrice’s agency visit; validation pending
**Observed visit:** 2026-07-27
**Recorded:** 2026-07-28

## Engineering position: immutable + append-only
Compliance records should preserve the original issued event and support controlled, attributable follow-on entries—not destructive edits and not an uncorrectable hardcoded record.

A future audit model should distinguish:
- immutable original issuance;
- append-only corrections, voids, refunds, reversals, or superseding records;
- actor, timestamp, reason, and source evidence for every event; and
- versioned legal-rule/template provenance.

## Reported operating issues
| Area | Reported condition | Week 1 validation / client follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Fees and payments | Client fees and payments are not clearly tracked. | Identify payment types, lifecycle, sources of truth, reconciliation process, responsible roles, and exceptions. |
| Agreements | Worker and requesting-employer contracts may not meet the standards required for a placement agency. | Obtain current forms and authoritative requirements; define approval, signature, version, storage, and renewal controls. |
| Financial visibility | Overhead spending is not clearly understood. | Request expense categories, source records, owner, cadence, and required management view. |
| Product/package performance | Top-selling product packages are not clearly known. | Request package definitions, sales records, pricing, time range, and success metric. |

## Evidence posture
These findings are field observations reported by Beatrice, not independently validated facts or legal conclusions. Supporting records—including the current business model canvas—are expected before scope, controls, or success measures are finalized.

## Immediate implication
The revised platform may need a financial/operational reporting foundation in addition to compliance workflows. Do not add that scope until the business model, source records, priorities, and seven-week delivery trade-offs are validated.
