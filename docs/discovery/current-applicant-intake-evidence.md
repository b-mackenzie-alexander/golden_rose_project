## Candidate onboarding implications — evidence, not approved scope
A future onboarding process should be staged rather than a direct digital copy of this one-page form:

1. **Account and minimal contact profile:** collect only needed contact information with field-level purpose and access rules.
2. **Worker preferences/availability:** role interests, live-in/out, schedule, language preference, and experience as reviewable profile data—not automatic eligibility or hiring decisions.
3. **References:** do not collect or contact reference information until the purpose, notice/consent, fields, retention, access, and contact procedure are approved. References contain third-party personal data and need their own controls.
4. **Attestation:** record the exact approved statement/template version, acceptance method, time, actor/account, and any localized-language variant. Do not reuse the pictured wording without legal approval.
5. **Communications:** record granular channel preference/consent, purpose, language, timestamp, source/version, and withdrawal/opt-out state. A single combined phone/text/WhatsApp/email checkbox is insufficient for a governed channel policy.
6. **Agency review:** represent completeness/review/approval/referral states explicitly, with attributable review notes and no automatic employment decision.
7. **Language parity:** English and Spanish are mandatory across this intake and every related worker/employer/staff flow. Offer explicit language selection and preserve the approved language/template version for governed content; do not treat the photographed bilingual wording as legally approved production translation.

## Privacy, security, and fairness constraints
- A precise home address is sensitive location data. Do not expose it to employers, other workers, or public pages; collect the least precision necessary and decide purpose/retention/access before adding it to onboarding.
- Live-in/out, caregiving role interest, availability, language, and reference information should be access-controlled and used only for approved matching/review purposes; avoid turning them into prohibited/proxy filters.
- The form does not justify adding government IDs, SSNs, criminal history, credit history, health information, biometrics, or background-screening data. Those require independent purpose, legal, vendor, minimization, security, and timing decisions.
- Worker data, consent/attestation events, references, and profile changes require role-based access, encryption at rest/in transit, audit logs, retention/deletion/legal-hold rules, and append-only historical evidence where issued/relied on.
