## What the canvas states
The canvas describes Golden Rose as a New York employment agency connecting qualified job seekers/dependable workers with employers. It characterizes the business as employer-paid recruitment and placement support: recruiting, screening, matching, interview coordination, placement support, and agreed placement services.

It names household employers, business employers, and job seekers as customer segments. The stated employer value proposition includes pre-screened candidates, bilingual communication, interview coordination, reference verification and hiring follow-up, community-based candidate access, written service terms, and urgent/hard-to-fill assistance. The job-seeker proposition emphasizes legitimate opportunities, respectful bilingual support, clear job/payment/location information, and interview/follow-up support.

The canvas identifies paper/manual operational activities that align with the office observations: job order intake, candidate screening/interviewing, verification, matching, employer interviews, placement confirmation, payment/receipt handling, follow-up, and compliance administration.

## Revenue-model evidence — not yet confirmed policy
The canvas presents the following possible employer-paid revenue structures:
- a flat placement fee, varied by position type, urgency, complexity, seniority, salary level, or guarantee period;
- a retained search fee with an initial retainer and remaining balance on milestones or hire;
- a monthly recruitment membership for employers with recurring hiring needs;
- volume/corporate recruitment agreements with customized project/per-hire pricing; and
- optional employer services, separately described and priced in the employer agreement.

It also contains a “Suggested Employer-Paid Service Packages” table: Essential Placement, Priority Search, Workforce Partner, and Volume Hiring. Its stated examples pair package features with a one-time flat fee, a higher flat fee or retainer plus success fee, membership plus reduced fee per hire, or customized project/per-hire pricing.

## Required interpretation boundary
Beatrice’s current reading—that the owner may be considering sliding-scale per-engagement contracts and static membership fees—is reasonable as a hypothesis from this canvas. It is not client-confirmed policy. Do not implement, advertise, charge, calculate, or enforce any package, price, discount, retainer, refund, membership, guarantee, or payment term until the client confirms it and applicable legal/contract review is complete.

## Build-planning implications
If pricing becomes in scope, the system should support a governed, effective-dated configuration model rather than hardcoded package names or rates:
- approved pricing/package versions and effective dates;
- explicit eligibility, fee basis, contract linkage, and approval authority;
- a traceable quote/agreement at the time of engagement;
- immutable issued invoices/receipts with append-only adjustments/refunds/voids; and
- reporting that separates quoted, invoiced, paid, refunded, waived, and outstanding amounts.

This is a planning constraint only—not an approval to build billing in V1.

## Other planning-relevant evidence
- Potential policy inconsistency requiring clarification: the canvas says job seekers should not be charged a placement fee, while the updated PRD specifies controls for recording applicant payments after confirmed placement. These could refer to different payment types, but they must not be reconciled by assumption.
- The canvas names Google/Maps-style location needs only indirectly through job location and community matching; it does not establish heat-map scope.
- It lists technology/CRM/website, candidate and employer relationship databases, contracts, job orders, interview forms, placement forms, and agency records as resources. This supports the need for a centralized source of truth but does not validate the quality or availability of current records.
- It lists legal counsel, insurance broker, bookkeeper, accountant, payment processor, and technology/reference-verification providers as potential partners. No vendor, data-sharing, or integration decision is confirmed.
- “Legal review required before implementation” appears on the canvas pages; that is consistent with the engagement’s compliance gate.

## Validation questions for the client
1. Which revenue structures are active today, which are intended future options, and which should the project support in the seven-week window?
2. For each active or proposed package: who is eligible, what is charged, when is it due, what triggers a refund/credit, and which signed agreement controls?
3. Are any worker/job-seeker fees charged? The canvas says job seekers should not be charged a placement fee; confirm actual practice and legal basis.
4. What financial evidence exists for quotes, agreements, invoices, receipts, payments, refunds, waivers, and aging balances?
5. Which performance indicators are decision-critical, and what reliable source data exists for them?
