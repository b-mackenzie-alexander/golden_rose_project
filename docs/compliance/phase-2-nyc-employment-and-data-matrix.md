## NYC employment-agency and job-posting controls

| ID | Authority / official source | Trigger / close paraphrase | Candidate product control | Counsel/client decision needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NYC-02 | [NYC Human Rights Law (NYCHRL), Admin. Code §§8-102, 8-107](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | A person regularly procuring employees or work opportunities can be an employment agency. The law prohibits an agency’s discriminatory receiving, classifying, referral, advertising, or compliance with discriminatory customer preferences. | Treat portal workflows, staff referral notes, job requests, filters, matching, and communications as agency conduct; prohibit protected-trait filters and route exception requests to a counsel-owned process. | Confirm entity/role facts and any narrow lawful BFOQ case; customer preference alone is not a sufficient basis. |
| NYC-03 | [NYCHRL §8-107](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | The NYCHRL protects actual or perceived protected status and bars discriminatory ads/statements/applications. | Do not expose protected status, apparent proxies, or unrestricted recruiter/client free text as matching criteria; moderate public/private job content; retain referral rationale and overrides. | Confirm current protected-category list and any legitimate, non-decisional demographic collection. |
| NYC-04 | [NYC Fair Chance Act / CCHR](https://www.nyc.gov/site/cchr/media/fair-chance-act.page), [NYCHRL §8-107(11-a)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | Before a conditional offer, an employer, employment agency, or agent generally may not ask, search for, receive, buy, or use criminal-history information; job ads may not state criminal-history limitations. | No criminal-history intake, search, vendor report, matching flag, or automatic exclusion before documented conditional offer; block “clean record/no felonies” ad text. | Confirm who makes conditional offer and any legally mandated screening/exemption. |
| NYC-05 | [NYC Fair Chance Act / CCHR](https://www.nyc.gov/site/cchr/media/fair-chance-act.page), [NYCHRL §8-107(11-a)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | A post-offer adverse action based in whole/part on criminal history generally requires report/inquiry copy, CCHR evaluation, at least five business days to respond, and vacancy hold during review. | Case workflow with immutable offer/report/notice events, CCHR-form version, response timer, secure response channel, vacancy hold, human decision, and final-notice record; prohibit vendor auto-denial. | Validate current CCHR form/version, delivery, exceptions, and FCRA interaction. |
| NYC-06 | [NYC Pay Transparency / CCHR](https://www.nyc.gov/site/cchr/media/pay-transparency.page), [NYCHRL §8-107(32)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | A covered NYC-reachable job ad must state the good-faith minimum and maximum annual salary or hourly wage. | Require pay floor/ceiling before publication; show it in listings, notifications, feeds, and shared job pages; preserve version history and employer attestation. | Confirm coverage of each local/remote placement and treatment of live-in compensation/benefits. |
| NYC-07 | [NYCHRL §8-107(25)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | Agencies generally may not ask/search for/use salary history in employment decisions, subject to limited exceptions. | Exclude prior-pay fields from onboarding, search, matching/rate suggestion, client view, and recruiter scripts. | Counsel to define voluntary-disclosure/other exceptions and interaction with agency fee calculations. |
| NYC-08 | [NYCHRL §8-107(24)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin) | Agencies generally may not request/use consumer credit history for employment decisions absent a specific statutory exemption. | No credit-check field/workflow; counsel-approved exemption attestation before any client request can proceed. | Determine whether a named role meets a real exemption; do not infer it from client preference or insurance. |

## Privacy, security, e-record, and vendor trigger controls

| ID | Authority / official source | Trigger / close paraphrase | Candidate product control | Counsel/client decision needed |

| DATA-02 | [NY GBL §899-aa (breach notice)](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/GBS/899-AA) | Covered NY private-information breach can require resident and regulator notice; method/timing depends on facts and statutory conditions. | Incident plan, breach evidence/decision log, owner–processor notification SLA, key/encryption facts, regulator-routing checklist, vendor clauses, tabletops. | Confirm breach facts, encryption/key state, resident count, ownership, law-enforcement delay, and other-regime overlap. |
| DATA-03 | [FCRA §§1681a, 1681b(b), 1681m(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title15/html/USCODE-2023-title15-chap41-subchapIII.htm) | A CRA-provided consumer report used for employment purposes can trigger disclosure/authorization/certification and pre-/post-adverse-action duties. | No background/identity/credit vendor integration until FCRA + NYC Fair Chance workflow is approved; store standalone authorization and notices; secure report copies; human adverse-action case flow. | Is vendor a CRA? Is returned data a consumer report? Who is user/decision maker? Is data used for employment action or only fraud/security verification? |
| DATA-04 | [FCRA §1681d](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title15/html/USCODE-2023-title15-chap41-subchapIII.htm) | A report based on personal interviews about character/reputation/lifestyle may be an investigative consumer report. | Vendor due diligence must identify interview/reference methods; add applicable disclosure/nature-and-scope response workflow before use. | Determine vendor methodology and report type. |
| DATA-05 | [E-SIGN Act §7001](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title15/html/USCODE-2023-title15-chap96.htm) | Electronic signatures/records in commerce are generally valid; when a legally required written disclosure is delivered electronically to a consumer, §7001(c) consent/access conditions apply; electronic retention must be accurate and accessible for the legally required period. | Capture signer intent/attribution, final document version, delivery/access evidence, download/print capability, and consumer-consent evidence where triggered. | Map each document/recipient to the writing/consumer/retention question; E-SIGN does not itself set retention duration or satisfy every NY form rule. |
| DATA-06 | [FTC Safeguards Rule, 16 CFR Part 314](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-314) | Conditional: the Rule applies to covered financial institutions; ordinary employment-agency payment processing does not alone establish coverage. | Use SHIELD-aligned controls as baseline; do not claim GLBA coverage/exemption until financial-activity and entity-role analysis is complete. | Determine lending, brokerage, insurance, escrow, money-transmission, or payment-facilitation roles beyond ordinary merchant acceptance. |

## Engineering guardrails added by Phase 2
1. Client inputs are never self-executing legal exceptions. The product must route protected-trait, criminal-history, credit-history, or BFOQ/exemption requests to trained human/counsel review.
2. Background reports are a gated, post-conditional-offer case type—not a generic candidate-search feature.
3. Job publication requires a verified job order, lawful-content review, and, where covered, a good-faith pay range.
4. Security/vendor controls are driven by a field-level data and decision-flow inventory, not a generic “encrypted” label.
5. Every legal/policy template, form, exception, notice, and rule must have an owner, version, effective date, source, and approval state.
