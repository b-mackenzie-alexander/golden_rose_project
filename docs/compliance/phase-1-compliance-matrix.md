# Compliance Matrix — Phase 1 Authoritative Research

**Status:** Research synthesis; not legal advice, legal review, or a compliance certification
**Research date:** 2026-07-28
**Product context:** Proposed NYC employment-agency portal for worker/employer onboarding, job orders, matching/referrals, documents, payments/receipts, and agency operations.

## How to use this matrix
“Candidate control” means an engineering requirement to assess and test after New York counsel/client confirms applicability. It is not approval to implement a legal rule, nor evidence that Golden Rose currently complies. Statutory sections cited below are official consolidated-law locations; official NY/NYC pages were intermittently access-blocked during research, so counsel must re-check current text/effective dates before release.

## Launch-blocking legal-review gates
1. **Agency and fee model:** Confirm Article 11/DCWP license status, licensed entity, bond/posting duties, and whether each employer-, worker-, subscription-, retainer-, or platform-fee model is a regulated agency fee.
2. **Applicant-fee policy:** Resolve the conflict between the canvas statement that job seekers do not pay placement fees and the PRD’s applicant-payment controls. Do not collect a worker/applicant fee until counsel approves the exact model.
3. **Domestic-worker taxonomy:** Approve the classification of each role (including household, live-in, home-care, casual/relative, and interstate-recruitment cases) before calculating a fee, wage, schedule, or document requirement.
4. **AI and matching:** No automated scoring, ranking, recommendation, or exclusion feature for NYC jobs/candidates until an AEDT and discrimination review is complete.
5. **Data and vendor architecture:** Complete data inventory, retention, breach-role, cloud/provider, identity, document, mapping, messaging, payment, and background-screening reviews before ingesting production personal data.

## Engineering controls common to the matrix
- Immutable original compliance events; append-only, linked correction/void/refund/superseding events.
- Effective-dated, counsel-approved legal/template/rate configuration; no hardcoded legal text or fee percentages.
- Evidence-grade lifecycle records for job order → referral → acceptance → start → invoice/receipt → payment/refund.
- Least privilege, MFA for staff, tenant/role isolation, encrypted transit/storage, audit logs, secure export, backup/recovery, retention/deletion, legal hold, and incident response.
- Explicit human review for matching/referral, exceptions, and employer-request legality.
