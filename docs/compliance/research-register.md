# Compliance Research Register — Golden Rose Employment Agency

**Status:** Week 1 research register; not legal advice and not a compliance certification
**Jurisdictional premise:** New York City employment agency serving domestic/household-worker placements, with a cloud-hosted worker/employer/agency portal under consideration.
**Last updated:** 2026-07-28

## Research operating rule
Only primary legal/regulatory sources, official agency guidance, or counsel-confirmed interpretations may become build requirements. Secondary commentary may identify questions but is not sufficient authority. Every candidate requirement needs: source URL/citation, version/effective date, applicability, product control, owner, verification method, and counsel/client decision status.

## Scope under review
1. NYC/DCWP employment-agency licensing, records, receipts, fees, disclosures, and enforcement.
2. New York State employment-agency, labor, domestic-worker, contract, payment, and privacy/security obligations.
3. Federal employment-discrimination, background-screening/consumer-reporting, electronic-signature, and privacy/security obligations.
4. Cross-cutting controls: identity verification, document handling, access control, auditability, communications, payment records, data retention/deletion, and incident response.

## Non-negotiable gates
- No product output may be called “legally compliant” until the governing rule, effective date, implementation, test evidence, and counsel/client approval are recorded.
- No legal text, fee cap, payment schedule, retention period, or disclosure template is hardcoded from planning documents or secondary sources.
- Compliance events use immutable original issuance plus attributable append-only corrections, voids, refunds, and superseding events.
- New third-party services (identity, maps, messaging, e-signature, payment, background screening) require a data-flow and contract/privacy review before use.

2. Record a plain-language obligation without extrapolation.
3. Map it to a proposed product behavior/control and test evidence.
